What is Monthly Bookkeeping?

Understanding the foundation of your financial strategy

Monthly bookkeeping is the systematic process of recording, categorizing, and reconciling all of your business's financial transactions on a monthly basis. Rather than waiting until tax season to scramble through a year's worth of receipts, a monthly bookkeeper ensures your financial data is always up-to-date, accurate, and ready for review. This ongoing financial maintenance is critical for tracking cash flow, monitoring profitability, and making informed strategic decisions.

At CountRights, we act as your dedicated virtual accounting department. We connect securely to your bank accounts and accounting software, categorize every transaction to the correct chart of accounts, reconcile your statements to the penny, and deliver monthly financial reports directly to your inbox. By outsourcing your outsourced bookkeeping to us, you gain peace of mind knowing your financials are expertly managed.

What's Included in Monthly Bookkeeping?

A comprehensive suite of solutions tailored for growing businesses

Bank & Credit Card Reconciliation

We reconcile your bank and credit card accounts to the penny every month. This process ensures that every transaction in your accounting software matches your actual bank statements, catching missing transactions, duplicate entries, or bank errors before they distort your financial picture.

Transaction Categorization

Every expense and income deposit is categorized to the correct chart of accounts. We ensure that your software accurately reflects your true operational costs, allowing you to see exactly where your money is going and identify potential tax deductions.

Monthly Financial Reporting

At the end of every month, you receive a clear, easy-to-read financial package. This includes your Profit & Loss (P&L) statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow statement, giving you a complete overview of your business's financial health.

Month-End Close Management

We handle the entire month-end close process. This involves reviewing variances, ensuring all adjusting entries are made, and finalizing your books so the previous month is locked and accurate, setting a clean slate for the next.

The Technology Behind Our Virtual Bookkeeping

Our virtual bookkeeping process is powered by best-in-class cloud technology. We utilize secure, read-only bank feeds to automatically import your daily transactions. This means we never need your actual bank passwords, and you maintain total control over your funds. We use advanced optical character recognition (OCR) technology for receipt and invoice capture, meaning you can simply snap a photo of a receipt on your phone, and our system will automatically extract the vendor, amount, and date, matching it to the corresponding transaction in your accounting software. This drastically reduces manual data entry and ensures every expense is documented for tax purposes.

For document storage and sharing, we use encrypted, SOC-compliant secure portals. You will never have to email sensitive financial documents as unsecured attachments. Everything is logged, tracked, and stored securely in the cloud. This level of technological integration allows us to work seamlessly with your internal team and your external CPA, ensuring everyone has access to the most up-to-date financial data in real-time. To keep your new file perfectly up to date, we highly recommend pairing your migration with our ongoing monthly bookkeeping services.
