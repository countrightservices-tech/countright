Sage to QuickBooks Conversion Experts

Professional ERP and desktop accounting migration services for growing US businesses

Transitioning from a robust system like Sage to QuickBooks is often a strategic move to reduce software costs, simplify daily operations, and improve user adoption for growing mid-sized businesses. CountRights provides professional Sage to QuickBooks migration services designed to extract your essential financial data and restructure it perfectly for QuickBooks. Whether you are moving to QuickBooks Online for team collaboration or QuickBooks Desktop for advanced inventory management, our team ensures a zero-data-loss conversion.

Migrating from Sage requires advanced data mapping. Sage’s multi-module structures, complex custom fields, and deep inventory matrices must be consolidated into QuickBooks' standard formats. Our accounting data conversion experts meticulously map your chart of accounts, subsidiary data, customer and supplier profiles, open invoices, unpaid bills, and historical financial transactions to ensure that your comparative reports remain perfectly accurate year-over-year.

One of the biggest challenges businesses face during an accounting software conversion is maintaining historical balances while simplifying the chart of accounts. We eliminate this issue by running a pre-migration assessment and a post-migration reconciliation. We compare your Profit and Loss (P&L) and Balance Sheet reports in Sage against your newly migrated QuickBooks file. This strict verification process guarantees that your financial history, account balances, and reporting continuity are preserved with 100% accuracy.

For businesses utilizing Sage 300 (Accpac) or Sage Intacct, the complexity often lies in multi-currency and multi-entity structures. Our team is highly experienced in consolidating these complex ledgers into a streamlined QuickBooks Online or QuickBooks Enterprise environment. We ensure that foreign exchange gains and losses are accurately calculated and that your opening balances reflect the correct translated amounts, giving you a true picture of your global financial health.

Why abandon legacy Sage desktop software? Desktop systems like Sage 50 require manual backups, remote desktop setups for outside CPAs, and lack real-time bank feed integrations. By moving to QuickBooks Online, your business gains the flexibility of cloud-based remote access, automated daily bank syncing, and seamless multi-user collaboration without the IT overhead. If you need ongoing help post-migration, our monthly bookkeeping services ensure your new file stays pristine.

We also understand that many businesses have been using Sage for decades. If you are running an older, discontinued version of Sage like Business Vision, Sage PFW, or Simply Accounting, you might think your data is trapped. It's not. We use advanced SQL extraction tools to pull raw data directly from the backend database, bypassing the locked user interface so we can recover your historical financials and migrate them to a modern platform. We can also help migrate your employee records and wage totals seamlessly if you are transitioning to our dedicated payroll services.

By transitioning to QuickBooks, your business gains access to advanced financial reporting, automated bank feeds, and a massive ecosystem of over 700 third-party integrations—without the heavy overhead of a full ERP. Don't let Sage software bloat slow down your business. Trust CountRights to securely handle your Sage to QuickBooks data migration so you can focus on running your business with clear, reliable financial data.
