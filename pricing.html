Accounting Software Migration Pricing

Custom flat-fee projects. No hourly billing surprises.

How Migration Pricing Works

Unlike hourly consultants who bill you for every email, CountRights quotes software migrations as a single flat fee. We analyze the volume of your historical data, the complexity of your chart of accounts, and whether you have multi-currency or inventory needs. You receive one upfront price. We guarantee zero data loss, and the price never changes.

  • Free Data Assessment: We review your current file for free before quoting.
  • 100% Data Accuracy: We map your historical GL, AR, and AP perfectly.
  • Zero Downtime: Keep using your old system while we build the new one.

Catch-Up Bookkeeping & Clean-Up Fees

One-time projects to get your books tax-ready.

Behind on your books?

If you are months or years behind on your bookkeeping, we offer one-time catch-up and clean-up services. We will reconcile past bank statements, reclassify improper expenses, and fix errors left by previous bookkeepers. This is billed as a flat project fee based on the number of months behind and the volume of transactions.

  • Tax-Ready Books: Hand clean P&L and Balance Sheets directly to your CPA.
  • Error Correction: We find and fix duplicate transactions and uncashed checks.
  • Seamless Transition: Once caught up, you can easily move to a low monthly plan.
