What is Forensic Accounting?

Forensic accounting is the specialized practice of investigating financial records to uncover fraud, embezzlement, or hidden assets. It combines accounting knowledge with investigative techniques, making it the "CSI" of the financial world. While a standard bookkeeper records transactions, a forensic accountant digs deep into the data to find out where the money really went, who took it, and how they covered their tracks.

At CountRights, we act as financial detectives. We trace missing funds, analyze lifestyle indicators, calculate business valuations, and provide expert witness testimony in court. Whether you are dealing with employee theft, a messy divorce, or a complex business dispute, our forensic accounting team provides the clarity and evidence you need to protect your interests and achieve a favorable legal outcome.

Warning Signs of Fraud

If you notice any of these red flags, contact us immediately for a confidential investigation:

  • Missing inventory or unexplained write-offs
  • An employee living beyond their means
  • Discrepancies between bank statements and books
  • Missing vendor invoices or duplicate payments
  • Refusal to take vacation by key staff

The Investigation Process

A systematic approach to uncovering financial truth
1

Discovery & Initial Assessment

We start with a confidential consultation to understand the situation, identify the suspected fraud or dispute, and determine what financial data is available. We establish the scope of the investigation.

2

Data Collection & Analysis

We collect bank statements, accounting files, and tax returns. Using advanced data analytics, we analyze thousands of transactions to identify anomalies, red flags, and suspicious patterns. If books are behind, we perform catch-up bookkeeping first.

3

Tracing & Quantification

We trace the flow of stolen funds, identify how the fraud was concealed, and calculate the exact financial damage. We also trace any hidden assets purchased with the stolen funds.

4

Reporting & Testimony

We prepare a detailed, defensible forensic report outlining our findings. If necessary, our experts provide testimony in court or during depositions to explain the financial evidence to the judge and jury.

The Anatomy of Occupational Fraud

Occupational fraud—fraud committed by employees against their employers—is a silent killer of small businesses. The most common schemes involve asset misappropriation, such as skimming cash, writing fraudulent checks, or submitting fake expense reports. Without a structured process, businesses easily fall victim to duplicate payments, missed early-payment discounts, and damaging late fees. When you outsource AP management to CountRights, we establish a rigorous, step-by-step workflow. We capture every vendor invoice, route it for approval, and enter it into your accounting software with precise coding. We track payment terms and schedule payments exactly when they are due, preserving your cash on hand.

Furthermore, managing payroll is a massive headache for most business owners. Calculating gross wages, withholding the correct taxes, dealing with garnishments, and filing quarterly payroll reports requires precision and deep knowledge of tax laws. A single mistake can result in hefty IRS penalties. Our integrated payroll services completely remove this burden. We ensure your employees are paid accurately and on time, and we seamlessly sync your payroll expenses directly into your general ledger. This integration provides a real-time, accurate view of your true labor costs, allowing you to make informed decisions about hiring and pricing. By tracking when money is coming in and when it needs to go out, we provide a clear cash flow forecast. This prevents overdrafts, ensures you have funds to pay vendors, and helps you collect from customers faster.

Business Valuation for Disputes and Buyouts

Determining the true value of a business is critical during a partnership dispute, buyout, or divorce. A business is not just worth its physical assets; its value includes goodwill, intellectual property, and future earning potential. CountRights performs comprehensive business valuations using established methodologies, such as the income approach, market approach, and asset-based approach. We analyze historical financial data, normalize earnings to remove owner perks, and calculate a defensible fair market value. This ensures that you receive a fair price whether you are buying out a partner or selling your company.

The Technology Behind Our Virtual Bookkeeping

Our virtual bookkeeping process is powered by best-in-class cloud technology. We utilize secure, read-only bank feeds to automatically import your daily transactions.
