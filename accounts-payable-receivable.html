What is AP & AR Management?

Accounts Payable (AP) is the money your business owes to vendors and suppliers for goods or services purchased on credit. Accounts Receivable (AR) is the money your customers owe you for products or services you have delivered but have not yet been paid for.

Managing these two cycles effectively is the lifeblood of your company. If AP gets out of hand, you face late fees and damaged vendor relationships. If AR slips, you run out of cash to pay your own bills. CountRights steps in as your virtual accounting department to meticulously track every invoice, schedule every payment, and collect every dollar, ensuring your outsourced bookkeeping runs flawlessly.

Accelerating Collections with Accounts Receivable (AR) Management

AP vs. AR: The Cash Flow Cycle

Understanding the difference between money going out and money coming in

Accounts Payable (AP) - Money Out

  • Vendor Invoices: Tracking bills for raw materials, software, and services.
  • Payment Scheduling: Ensuring bills are paid on time to avoid late fees.
  • Expense Tracking: Categorizing outgoing cash accurately for tax deductions.
  • 1099 Prep: Tracking contractor payments for year-end tax reporting.

Accounts Receivable (AR) - Money In

  • Customer Invoicing: Generating and sending accurate billing statements.
  • Payment Matching: Matching incoming deposits to open invoices.
  • Collections: Sending reminders for past-due accounts to accelerate cash flow.
  • Revenue Tracking: Monitoring which products/services are driving income.
