Wave to QuickBooks Conversion Experts

Professional accounting migration services for growing businesses

Wave is an excellent free platform for freelancers and new businesses just getting started. However, as your company grows, you often outgrow its basic income and expense tracking. CountRights provides professional Wave to QuickBooks migration services designed to seamlessly transition your business into a more robust, true double-entry accounting ecosystem. Whether you are moving to QuickBooks Online for team collaboration or QuickBooks Desktop for advanced inventory management, our team ensures a zero-data-loss conversion.

Migrating from Wave requires precise data mapping. Because Wave lacks a traditional double-entry chart of accounts, translating its simple income/expense categories into QuickBooks' full accounting structure requires expert handling. Our accounting data conversion experts meticulously map your customer profiles, outstanding invoices, tracked expenses, product lists, and historical financial transactions to ensure that your comparative reports remain perfectly accurate year-over-year.

One of the biggest challenges businesses face during an accounting software conversion is mismatched opening balances and lost historical data. We eliminate this issue by running a pre-migration assessment and a post-migration reconciliation. We compare your Profit and Loss (P&L) and Balance Sheet reports in Wave against your newly migrated QuickBooks file. This strict verification process guarantees that your financial history, account balances, and reporting continuity are preserved with 100% accuracy. Once your migration is complete, we highly recommend pairing it with our ongoing monthly bookkeeping services to keep your new QuickBooks file perfectly up to date.

By upgrading to QuickBooks, your business gains access to advanced financial reporting, robust inventory management, automated bank feeds, and a massive ecosystem of over 700 third-party integrations. Don't let basic software limit your business growth. Trust CountRights to securely handle your Wave to QuickBooks data migration so you can focus on running your business with clear, reliable financial data.
